Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Repeat Wise ("the App") is developed by Some Simple Solutions. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data That Stays on Your Device

All of your personal content is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Tasks, tags, and completion history
  • Diary entries, mood selections, color choices, and diary reflections
  • Voice notes and videos you record

The Repeat Wise widget and its Siri & Shortcuts actions read this same data on your device. We do not have access to it. If you delete the App, this data is permanently removed.

On-Device Intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the App can use Apple's on-device model for features such as creating a task from a description, reflecting on a diary entry, suggesting a mood, searching by meaning, and schedule insights. This processing happens entirely on your device. Your content is not sent to us or to any third party. You can turn these features off at any time in Settings > Intelligence.

Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage data such as which screens are viewed and which features are used. This helps us understand how the App is used so we can improve it. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. Analytics is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share Analytics.

Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to collect crash reports. When the App crashes, technical information about the crash (device model, operating system version, and stack trace) is sent automatically. This data does not include your personal content and is used solely to diagnose and fix issues.

For more information on how Google processes this data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy documentation.

Camera and Microphone

The App requests access to your camera and microphone solely to let you record videos and voice notes for your diary entries. Media you capture is stored on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

No Tracking

The App does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not share any data with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service the App communicates with is Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). No other external services receive data from the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
