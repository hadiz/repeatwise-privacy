Frequently Asked Questions

What is Repeat Wise?

Repeat Wise helps you manage recurring tasks and habits with a warm, distraction-free interface. Create tasks that repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, organize them with tags, and reflect on your day with a built-in diary.

Which devices does Repeat Wise support?

Repeat Wise runs on iPhone (iOS 26 and later) and iPad (iPadOS 26 and later). Native Mac and Apple Vision Pro versions are on our roadmap.

Is my data backed up or synced to the cloud?

No. All of your tasks, tags, completions, and diary entries are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed.

Can I export my data?

Data export is on our roadmap. If this is important to you, please email us — user requests directly shape what we build next.

How do recurring tasks work?

When you create a task, choose a recurrence (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or none). The task will appear on the Today tab on each scheduled date, and missed or completed history is visible on the History tab.

How do I edit or delete a task?

Open the Manage tab to see all tasks. Tap a task to edit it, or swipe left to delete. Deleting a task also removes its completion history.

How do tags work?

Tags help you group related tasks (e.g., Work, Health, Home). Open the Manage tab and tap the tag icon in the toolbar to view or edit your tags. You can assign one or more tags to any task.

Can I attach photos, voice notes, or videos to diary entries?

Yes. From the Diary tab, you can record a voice note or add a video to any entry. Captured media is stored on your device only.

Does Repeat Wise have a Home Screen widget?

Yes. Long-press your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for "Repeat Wise," and add the widget to see your tasks at a glance.

Can I use Siri to add tasks?

Yes. Try saying "Add task in Repeat Wise" or "Create daily task in Repeat Wise" and Siri will open the app's Add Task screen.

How do I change the start of the week, theme, or haptics?

Tap the gear icon on the Today tab to open Settings. From there you can choose your week start day, switch the warm theme, and toggle haptic feedback.

Is Repeat Wise accessible?

Yes. The app supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and high-contrast appearances. If you encounter any accessibility issue, please reach out — we treat these reports as a high priority.

Does Repeat Wise track me or show ads?

No. Repeat Wise has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. We collect only anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to improve stability. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting Repeat Wise from your device permanently removes all of your tasks, tags, completions, and diary entries.

Reporting a Bug

If something isn't working as expected, please email us with:

  • A short description of what happened
  • The steps you took before the issue appeared
  • Your device model and iOS / macOS / visionOS version
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

The more detail you can share, the faster we can fix it.
