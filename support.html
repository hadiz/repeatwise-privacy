Frequently Asked Questions

What is Repeat Wise?

Repeat Wise helps you manage recurring tasks and habits with a warm, distraction-free interface. Create tasks that repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, organize them with tags, and reflect on your day with a built-in diary.

Which devices does Repeat Wise support?

Repeat Wise runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 and later. Mac and Apple Vision Pro versions are on our roadmap.

Which languages is Repeat Wise available in?

English, German, and French. Repeat Wise follows your device's language. To use a different language just for Repeat Wise, open Settings in the app and tap Language, which takes you to the app's page in the system Settings.

Is my data backed up or synced to the cloud?

No. All of your tasks, tags, completions, and diary entries are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed.

Can I export my data?

Data export is on our roadmap. If this is important to you, please email us — user requests directly shape what we build next.

How do recurring tasks work?

When you create a task, choose how often it repeats: once, daily, on weekdays or weekends, weekly, monthly, yearly, or on specific dates. The task appears on the Today tab on each scheduled date, and completed or missed tasks are visible on the History tab.

How do I edit or delete a task?

Open the Manage tab to see all tasks. Tap a task to edit it, or swipe left to delete. Deleting a task also removes its completion history.

How do tags work?

Tags help you group related tasks (e.g., Work, Health, Home). Open the Manage tab and tap the tag icon in the toolbar to view or edit your tags. You can assign one or more tags to any task.

Can I add voice notes or videos to diary entries?

Yes. From the Diary tab, you can record a voice note or a video for any entry. Captured media is stored on your device only.

What are the smart features, and which devices have them?

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Repeat Wise can turn a plain-language description into a task, reflect on a diary entry with a short summary and key points, suggest a mood as you write, search your tasks and diary by meaning, and offer Insights about your schedule in the History tab. Everything runs on your device, and nothing is sent to us. To turn these features off, open Settings in the app and switch off Use On-Device Intelligence. On other devices, Repeat Wise works fully without them.

Does Repeat Wise have a Home Screen widget?

Yes. Long-press your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for "Repeat Wise," and add the widget to see your tasks at a glance. It follows the theme you choose in the app.

Can I use Siri to add tasks?

Yes. Try saying "Add task in Repeat Wise" or "Describe a task in Repeat Wise". Siri asks what to add and creates the task. You'll also find Repeat Wise actions in the Shortcuts app.

How do I change the start of the week, theme, or haptics?

Tap the gear icon on the Today tab to open Settings. From there you can choose your week start day and weekend days, pick a theme, toggle haptic feedback, and change the app's language.

Is Repeat Wise accessible?

Yes. The app supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and high-contrast appearances. If you encounter any accessibility issue, please reach out — we treat these reports as a high priority.

Does Repeat Wise track me or show ads?

No. Repeat Wise has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. We collect only anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to improve stability, and you can turn analytics off in Settings > Privacy. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting Repeat Wise from your device permanently removes all of your tasks, tags, completions, and diary entries.

Reporting a Bug

If something isn't working as expected, please email us with:

  • The steps you took before the issue appeared

  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

The more detail you can share, the faster we can fix it.
